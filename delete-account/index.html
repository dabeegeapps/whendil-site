Delete Your WHENDIL Account

You can delete your account directly from the app:

  1. Open WHENDIL
  2. Go to Settings
  3. Scroll to Account Options
  4. Tap Delete Account and confirm

This permanently deletes your account and all associated data, including your trackers, completion history, and categories. This action cannot be undone.

Can't access the app?
Email us at dabeege.apps@gmail.com and we'll delete your account manually within 30 days.
