Privacy Policy

WHENDIL — Last updated July 9, 2026

WHENDIL ("we", "our", or "the app") is a simple elapsed-time tracker that helps you remember when you last did things. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

Information We Collect

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Email address — used to identify your account and send magic link sign-in emails.
  • Name and profile picture — only if you sign in with Google or Apple and those services share them. We do not require or display these.

When you use the app, we store:

  • Tracker data — the names of your trackers, completion timestamps, reminder settings, categories, and priority flags you create.
  • App preferences — theme, accent color, notification mode, and other settings stored on your device.

We also collect:

  • Crash reports — if the app crashes, anonymous diagnostic data is sent to Sentry to help us fix bugs. This does not include your tracker content.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide the app's core functionality (storing and syncing your trackers).
  • To send local push notifications for reminders you configure.
  • To diagnose and fix crashes and bugs.

We do not sell your data. We do not use your data for advertising.

Third-Party Services

  • Supabase — our backend database and authentication provider. Your account and tracker data are stored on Supabase servers. See supabase.com/privacy.
  • Google Sign-In — optional sign-in method on Android and web. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Apple Sign In — optional sign-in method on iOS and web. See Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • Sentry — crash reporting. See sentry.io/privacy.
